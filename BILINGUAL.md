# Bilingual site (EN/FR)

## How it works
- All public text lives in `lib/i18n/dictionaries.ts` (English and Canadian French). Edit wording there only.
- The visitor's language is kept in the `adlc_lang` cookie. First visit: `?lang=fr` or `?lang=en` in the link wins, otherwise the browser language.
- EN/FR button in the header (desktop and mobile).
- Shareable French link: `https://adlcoalition.org/?lang=fr`
- Membership alerts from the French site arrive with `[FR]` in the email subject.
